Programme photographs

The five photographs used on our Impact page were supplied by Neorons on 27 October 2026. Images retain their original content, with responsive framing for different screens. Figures shown with them describe overall programme results, not the individuals or single sessions pictured.

India programme archive

Illustrative images retained from the original Neorons website have individual creator, source and licence links beneath each photograph in the India programme archive. These images illustrate programme themes; they do not document the events described.

Supporter logos

Official artwork is sourced from the organisations’ own websites: Google Brand Resource Center, Amazon Press Center, Microsoft, UNICEF, UNDP design system, OpenAI, Meta, UNFPA, and the Government of India’s Ministry of Home Affairs. All marks remain the property of their respective organisations. Support relationships are described by Neorons.

Campus logos

The 23 IIT and 22 IIM marks are sourced from official institutional websites and the Government of India’s National Informatics Centre logo directory. Each mark on the campus programmes section links to its institution. These marks identify campuses where Neorons reports programme work; they are distinct from the supporter groups.
