About Neorons

Neorons is a nonprofit focused on suicide prevention. Its work has reached more than 500,000 people across over 80 countries. Its novel multilingual conversational AI phone service reaches 125,000 people. Neorons also runs inclusive campus and community programmes.

Key figures

  • 500,000+people reached across more than 80 countries
  • 125,000people reached through our multilingual AI phone service
  • 23 IIT · 22 IIMcampuses with inclusive programmes
  • US$75,000+raised for suicide prevention
