Campus institution roster — checked 27 October 2026

File: campus-roster.json
Scope: 23 IIT institutions + 22 IIM institutions, 45 unique entries. These numbers count institutions, not every physical, satellite, extension, international, or temporary campus. Each institution can operate more than one campus. The short names retain official historic branding (IIT Bombay/Madras, IIM Bangalore/Calcutta); city labels use the associated current city or main locality. City is a display label, not an event venue or precise geographic location.

PRIMARY SOURCES

1. JoSAA 2026, Ministry of Education: https://josaa.nic.in/josaa/
The 2026–27 admissions introduction explicitly states 23 IITs.

2. JoSAA 2026 institute directory: https://josaa.admissions.nic.in/applicant/seatmatrix/instituteview.aspx
The first 23 entries, institute codes 101–123, list every IIT with its address and official website. This is the source for the IIT roster and URLs. Location notes: IIT Bombay is in Mumbai; IIT Madras in Chennai; IIT Delhi in New Delhi; IIT Ropar in Rupnagar; IIT Goa in Farmagudi, Ponda; IIT Bhilai in Kutelabhata, Durg; IIT Hyderabad in Kandi, Sangareddy. Other city labels are institutional urban/district identities rather than street addresses (e.g. IIT Bhubaneswar's main campus is in Argul, Khurda).

3. Official CAT 2025 Information Bulletin, hosted by IIM Ahmedabad: https://www.iima.ac.in/sites/default/files/2025-08/Information_Bulletin.pdf
Pages 1–2 list the 21 IIMs then participating, including IIM Mumbai, with official website addresses. This provides the base IIM roster and its URLs. It predates the operational launch of IIM Guwahati.

4. IIM Guwahati, official institute page: https://www.iimg.ac.in/the-institute/
Identifies IIM Guwahati as the 22nd IIM, established under the Ministry of Education, located in Guwahati, Assam, and mentored by IIM Ahmedabad.

5. IIM Guwahati, official admission process: https://www.iimg.ac.in/admission-process/
States that it was inaugurated on 14 February 2026 and describes its inaugural MBA 2026–28 admission process.

6. IIM Guwahati, official home page: https://www.iimg.ac.in/
Announces inauguration of the first MBA batch on 21 July 2026 and gives its current address as Tech City, Bongora, Kamrup, Assam. The homepage occasionally timed out in direct retrieval but was indexed with this text. Source 4 was directly retrieved successfully.

7. PIB, Prime Minister's Office, 13 February 2026: https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2227367&lang=1&reg=6
The Government of India announced the IIM Guwahati inauguration during the Prime Minister's 14 February Assam visit. Corroborates the official institute account.

8. IIM Ahmedabad institutional announcement: https://www.iima.ac.in/the-institute/administration/work-with-us/IIMGuwahati
Records IIM Guwahati's establishment announcement in 2025 and planned first academic session in 2026–27. Consequently, distinguish the 2025 legal establishment/announcement from the 2026 inauguration and operational first batch; do not claim all of these occurred on one date.

9. IIM Sirmaur official home page: https://www.iimsirmaur.ac.in/
The footer identifies its permanent campus at Dhaula Kuan, District Sirmaur, and separately links a city campus at Paonta Sahib. Roster city label is therefore 'Dhaula Kuan, Sirmaur', not a claim that Sirmaur is a city.

10. Ministry of Education IIM Bill schedule: https://www.education.gov.in/sites/upload_files/mhrd/files/upload_document/IIM_Bill_2015_for_%20consultation.pdf
Maps the historic institute names IIM Bangalore/Calcutta to current city names Bengaluru/Kolkata. Used only for this stable naming distinction, not current institution counts.

All URLs in the JSON are official institution sites listed by the above primary sources, normalized to HTTPS. This does not assert that every external server responds reliably at all times. No institution event, Neorons affiliation, participant count, outcome, testimonial, programme date, or photograph was verified by this roster research. Neorons' reported work across the institutions remains organization-supplied information.
