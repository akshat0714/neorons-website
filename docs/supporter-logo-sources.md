## Brand-use caveats

Authentic downloadable artwork does not itself establish endorsement or grant permission for a third-party partner/sponsor presentation. Keep each logo tied to the precise relationship supplied by Neorons; do not broaden the existing claims.

- Google sponsorship guidance: https://about.google/brand-resource-center/guidance/sponsorships/ . Its guidance describes approval through the Google contact, unmodified full-colour artwork on light backgrounds, equal prominence within a sponsor tier, and clear space. Google for Nonprofits or Ad Grants participation alone is explicitly distinguished from sponsorship.
- Amazon's Press Center makes these assets publicly available. Its images-and-videos page directs non-press users to its trademark information: https://press.aboutamazon.com/images-and-videos . Preserve the downloaded artwork and existing relationship scope.
- UNESCO's FAQ states that its name and logo require prior written authorization: https://www.unesco.org/en/faq . Its graphical standards distinguish different identity blocks; avoid substituting a national commission or institute logo for the organization: https://www.unesco.org/sites/default/files/medias/fichiers/2023/10/outside_toolkit_20221129_eng.pdf .
- UNDP's brand guidance requires official artwork and preservation of the emblem/letter relationship: https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2021-08/UNDP%20Brand%20Manual%20rev.2020.pdf . Its partner-use guidance describes written consent: https://healthimplementation.undp.org/functional-areas/legal-framework/other-legal-and-implementation-considerations/logos-and-other-markings/ .
- No independent authorization records were located or asserted in this sourcing task. The files and sources are prepared for the user's requested design; no HTML/CSS or publication actions were made by this subtask.
