# IIT logo sources

Retrieved and checked: 27 October 2026.

## Scope and institutional roster

All 23 Indian IIT institutions are represented once. Institution names and official domains were validated against the Government of India Joint Seat Allocation Authority (JoSAA) 2026 directory, institute codes 101–123:

[JoSAA 2026 institute directory](https://josaa.admissions.nic.in/applicant/seatmatrix/instituteview.aspx). Overseas, satellite and extension campuses are not additional institutions in this collection.

The matching machine-readable manifest is `work/iit-logos.json` in the preparation workspace. Assets are self-hosted in `images/campuses/`. Artwork was downloaded without changing colours, proportions or content. Local filename extensions match the decoded file format. No generated marks, substitute symbols, Wikimedia files, or remote runtime image dependencies are used.

## Source exceptions and selection

- **IIT Gandhinagar:** direct retrieval from the institute homepage and official alternate subdomains returned a Cloudflare challenge. The logo was obtained instead from the public preview in the Government of India National Informatics Centre (NIC) UXDT repository. Login-protected download links were not used.
- **IIT Roorkee:** the official institute homepage embeds its institute wordmark from `cmsredesign.channeli.in`, the Channeli CMS asset host. That exact linked asset is used.
- Official header logos that were white on transparent backgrounds were replaced with readable institute marks from the same institute’s other official pages or assets. A Roorkee international-relations departmental candidate was rejected and is not included.

## Selection notes

- **IIT (ISM) Dhanbad:** Official homepage institute emblem; selected instead of the white header wordmark.
- **IIT Delhi:** Full institute logo displayed by the official IIT Delhi Central Library; selected instead of the white homepage variant.
- **IIT Gandhinagar:** Official Government of India National Informatics Centre UXDT logo repository. Institute homepage and official alternate subdomains returned a Cloudflare challenge; publicly displayed NIC logo preview used without accessing login-protected download options.
- **IIT Goa:** Full-resolution 497 × 512 institute crest published in the official homepage metadata; the smaller browser-icon variants were not used.
- **IIT Jammu:** Source image is named in the official site bundle main-WRS5KTIN.js for the /institute-logo page.
- **IIT Jodhpur:** Official Institute Logo page; selected instead of the white header variant.
- **IIT Roorkee:** Institute wordmark embedded directly by the official IIT Roorkee homepage; asset is hosted on its Channeli CMS domain.
- **IIT Ropar:** This 100 × 100 full institute crest is used by the primary navigation in the official homepage bundle index-CnqQR82e.js, as well as by its browser icon link. It is the legible institution crest, not a generic favicon substitute.
- **IIT Tirupati:** Source SVG is used in the official site header and identified in main.0fd73422.js; the /aboutlogo page explains the institute mark.

## Validation

- Manifest has 23 unique institutions and 23 existing assets, matching the JoSAA roster.
- All raster files were decoded and verified with Pillow. Total source asset size: 3,121,467 bytes.
- SHA-256 checksums and intrinsic dimensions are recorded in the manifest.
- Raster marks were visually inspected against a white background. White or inappropriate departmental variants were rejected.
- SVG checks:
  - IIT Delhi: XML parsed; no scripts, foreignObject, event handlers, image embeds, external references, imports, or external entities. Artwork consists of paths (plus structural metadata for Delhi).
  - IIT Tirupati: XML parsed; no scripts, foreignObject, event handlers, image embeds, external references, imports, or external entities. Artwork consists of paths (plus structural metadata for Delhi).

Institution identities and artwork provenance are documented here. These sources do not independently establish Neorons’ activities or institutional endorsement.
