## Verification

The licensing and original captions were checked against the Commons file records using their imageinfo/extmetadata API. The Australian team, Māori workshop and Gjoa Haven records were also opened in the web tool. Te Papa’s own article independently confirms the Māori photo credit and context. All four downloaded files were opened and visually inspected. A faraway Pangnirtung outdoor-learning shot and a Sámi portrait were rejected in favour of these clearer group/activity photographs.
