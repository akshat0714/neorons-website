## California SB 1318

The California photograph concerns **SB 1318 from the 2023–2024 session**, authored by Senator Aisha Wahab: “Pupil health: suicide prevention policies: pupil suicide crisis.” It is distinct from the five federal proposals.

The bill became **Chapter 645, Statutes of 2024**, approved and filed on **September 27, 2024**. It provides for school suicide-crisis intervention protocols that prioritise mental-health professionals. The page identifies the photographed advocacy and the law’s enactment separately; it does not claim Neorons authored or caused passage of this law.

Sources:

- [Governor of California signing announcement, September 27, 2024](https://www.gov.ca.gov/2024/09/27/governor-newsom-signs-legislation-to-strengthen-mental-health-and-substance-use-disorder-care-for-all-californians/)
- [California Department of Justice copy of enacted text](https://oag.ca.gov/system/files/media/sb882-101824-agenda-3b.pdf)

Do not substitute the 2025–2026 session’s SB 1318, which addresses a different subject. The screenshot alone does not establish the date of Akshat’s appearance, so no hearing date is claimed on the page.
