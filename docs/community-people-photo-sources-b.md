# Additional community photographs

These photographs document the independent activities named in their captions. None shows Neorons delivering a service, a clinical encounter, or the people pictured experiencing distress. They provide community context; the pictured people are not evidence for suicide statistics or participants in the separate health initiatives discussed on the page.

All four images were visually inspected. Files are unaltered Wikimedia Commons 1280-pixel derivatives, with their original proportions retained. Display crops should keep the people and activity legible. Keep the creator, source link and licence accessible from the public photo credits. No image was generated or reconstructed.
