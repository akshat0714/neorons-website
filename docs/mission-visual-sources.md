# Mission visual update · November 14, 2026

The main narrative uses large community photographs, short factual captions and an eight-context globe. Evidence, legal references and the existing conceptual phone experience are available through accessible native disclosures. Photographs depict independent community life, not Neorons programme delivery, callers, patients or health outcomes.

## Evidence

The short public statement is qualified: some Indigenous populations face disproportionately high suicide rates. It does not rank communities globally or imply that every Indigenous population has elevated mortality. The 2011–2016 Canadian cohort and Australia’s 2025–2035 strategy support the scope and community-led prevention principles. Dates and limitations remain visible in the evidence disclosure.

- [Statistics Canada: suicide among First Nations, Métis and Inuit, 2011–2016](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/pub/99-011-x/99-011-x2019001-eng.htm)
- [Australian Government: National Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander Suicide Prevention Strategy 2025–2035](https://www.health.gov.au/resources/publications/national-aboriginal-and-torres-strait-islander-suicide-prevention-strategy)
