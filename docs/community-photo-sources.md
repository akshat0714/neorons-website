# Community context photographs

Verified and downloaded 14 November 2026. These are real photographs of places, selected for geographic and community context. They do **not** document Neorons programme delivery, service use, partnerships, or any individual's health. No identifiable portraits were selected. The locations below are specific examples within the named regions, not representations of every community in a region.

The local JPEGs are Wikimedia Commons' 1280-pixel renditions, downloaded without additional editing. Responsive CSS framing may crop them in the website layout.
