Our people

Our team

Founded and led by students and recent graduates.

Founder & Executive Director

Akshat Agarwal

Akshat founded Neorons, sets programme direction, and builds school and partner relationships. His background in AI and suicidology, including published research in machine learning and bioethics, guides Neorons’ safety-first approach to technology in education.

Co-founder & Technology Lead

Avinash Amanchi

Avinash leads technology and curriculum, including Arduino kits, robot challenges, AI-literacy materials and mentor guides. He studies data science and builds accessibility software, including a communication tool for people with aphasia. He also manages registration data and impact measurement.

Co-founder & Lead Financial Advisor

Abhinav Gangadari

Abhinav oversees financial strategy, budgets, programme funding and capital management. He manages resource allocation and costs across Neorons’ programmes.
