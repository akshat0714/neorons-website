Our work in India.

Community projects in suicide prevention, disability inclusion, and education.

Eight completed projects and three originally planned projects. Planned dates reflect the original announcements and do not confirm delivery.

11 projects: 8 completed, 3 originally planned.

STEM for Social Good

Completed

Mind Matters Hackathon

A 48-hour hackathon where student teams built technology for suicide prevention and early mental-health support, guided by clinicians and engineers.

Bengaluru Urban, Karnataka12–14 September 2025
