02 / What you’ll work on

Skills and support

The proposed programme combines coaching, practical skills, mentorship and peer learning.

Career coaching

Set career goals

Explore roles and build a plan around your strengths and goals.

Training & skills

Practise new skills

Practise presenting ideas and identify the technical and professional skills you want to develop.

Industry mentorship

Meet professionals

Ask professionals about their work and career decisions.

Interview preparation

Prepare for interviews

Practise interview questions and use feedback to explain your experience clearly.

Peer community

Learn with peers

Share ideas, experiences and feedback in a small group.

Career connections

Build your network

Find people and organisations relevant to your goals and practise reaching out.

03 / Proposed programme

Six-week outline

  1. Week 01

    Set a goal

    Meet the group and choose a career goal.

  2. Week 02

    CV & introduction

    Work on your CV and personal introduction.

  3. Week 03

    Practical skills

    Complete an activity linked to your career interests.

  4. Week 04

    Interview practice

    Try interview questions and exchange feedback.

  5. Week 05

    Explore roles

    Explore roles and further learning opportunities.

  6. Week 06

    Make a plan

    Review your progress and set your next steps.
