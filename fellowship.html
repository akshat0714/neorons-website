01 / Who it’s for

Room for your
ambition.

Moving from study into work can bring questions about skills, confidence, and access. You deserve the opportunity to explore those questions with support.

The Neorons Fellowship is being designed for university students and early-career professionals with visible or non-visible disabilities, across professional disciplines.

It brings our commitment to inclusion into a practical space for learning, connection, and the next step in your career.

02 / What you’ll work on

Build the skills.
Find your next step.

The proposed programme brings coaching, skills practice, mentorship, and peer learning into one shared six-week experience.

Career coaching

A plan built
around you.

Guided conversations to reflect on your strengths, explore possible roles, and shape a practical plan around your goals.

Training & skills

Learn through
doing.

Develop practical career skills, from presenting your ideas to identifying the technical and professional learning that supports your direction.

Industry mentorship

Learn from
experience.

A space to hear from professionals, ask practical questions about their work, and learn from the choices that shaped their careers.

Interview preparation

Practise the
conversation.

Prepare your career story, try interview questions, and use feedback to communicate your experience and strengths more clearly.

Peer community

Learn alongside
other fellows.

Share ideas and experiences in a small group. Make room for different perspectives and support each other’s progress.

Career connections

Explore paths
into work.

Learn how to build a professional network, reach out with purpose, and identify people and organisations relevant to your goals.

03 / The proposed six-week journey

One step.
Then the next.

A shared structure, with space to connect what you learn to your own goals.

  1. Week 01

    Find your starting point

    Meet the group, reflect on your strengths, and choose a goal to work towards.

  2. Week 02

    Tell your story

    Bring your experience into focus through your CV and a clear personal introduction.

  3. Week 03

    Put a skill into practice

    Work through a practical activity connected to your interests and career direction.

  4. Week 04

    Practise the conversation

    Try interview questions, exchange feedback, and make space for another attempt.

  5. Week 05

    Explore your options

    Look at possible roles, learning opportunities, and questions to ask as you move forward.

  6. Week 06

    Plan what comes next

    Reflect on your progress and put together an action plan for your next chapter.
