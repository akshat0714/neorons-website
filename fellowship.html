02 / Learning and support

Programme areas

The proposed curriculum covers six areas of career preparation and peer learning.

01

Career coaching

Participants explore roles and develop a plan based on their interests and strengths.

02

Professional skills

Activities cover presenting ideas and identifying technical and professional skills to develop.

03

Industry mentorship

Conversations with professionals cover their work and career decisions.

04

Interview preparation

Practice interviews and feedback help participants describe their experience clearly.

05

Peer learning

Small groups exchange ideas, experiences and feedback.

06

Career networks

Participants identify relevant people and organisations and practise professional introductions.

03 / Proposed programme

Six-week outline

  1. Week 01

    Goals and introductions

    Participants meet the group and choose a career goal.

  2. Week 02

    CV preparation

    Participants develop a CV and personal introduction.

  3. Week 03

    Skills practice

    An activity connects practical skills with career interests.

  4. Week 04

    Interview practice

    Practice interviews include questions and peer feedback.

  5. Week 05

    Career options

    Participants review roles and further learning opportunities.

  6. Week 06

    Next steps

    A final review identifies progress and next steps.
