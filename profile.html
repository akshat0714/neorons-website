Organization profile

Neorons

Suicide prevention, across languages and borders.

Neorons is a nonprofit advancing suicide prevention through conversation, technology, and inclusion. We bring together multilingual access, campus programs, and community partnerships to reach people across the world.

500,000+people reached
80+countries reached
125,000people reached through our AI phone service
US$75,000+raised for suicide prevention

Our work

Multilingual conversations. Our novel conversational AI phone service brings multilingual access into our suicide-prevention work. It is supported by Google, Amazon, and Microsoft.

Inclusive campuses. We have worked across all 23 IIT campuses and 22 IIM campuses, running inclusive programs.

Community programs. Education, wellbeing, technology, and inclusion come together through local partnerships. Our programs are supported by UNICEF, UNESCO, and UNDP.

Our India camps and events are documented separately at neorons.com/india.html.
