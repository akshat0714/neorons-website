Our work

Multilingual AI phone service. Our novel conversational AI phone service reaches 125,000 people in multiple languages. It is supported by Google, Amazon, and Microsoft.

Inclusive campuses. We have worked across all 23 IIT campuses and 22 IIM campuses, running inclusive programs.

Community programs. We work with local partners on education, wellbeing, technology, and inclusion. Our programs are supported by UNICEF, UNESCO, and UNDP.

Our India camps and events are documented separately at neorons.com/india.html.
