Website information updated: October 2026

Our approach

Neorons is committed to making this website accessible to people with disabilities. This website aims to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA.

Design features

  • Keyboard access: Navigation and interactive controls use links and buttons, with visible keyboard focus.
  • Clear structure: Headings and page landmarks organize content for navigation and assistive technology.
  • Skip link: A “Skip to main content” link lets visitors move past the repeated navigation.
  • Readable content: The design uses contrasting text and backgrounds, clear type, and responsive layouts.
  • Images: Meaningful images include descriptive alternative text.
  • Motion: Reduced-motion preferences are respected. No audio or video plays automatically.
  • Decorative motion follows your device’s reduced-motion setting. Use “Pause motion” on the homepage to stop animation; your choice is remembered in this browser.

Contact

Email links open your email application. If that does not work on your device, copy neoronsai@gmail.com into your preferred email service.

Reporting barriers

If you experience any difficulty accessing this website, please contact us at neoronsai@gmail.com. We take every report seriously and will work to resolve barriers promptly. If possible, include the page and a brief description of the issue.
