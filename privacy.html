Policy

Privacy policy

How this website handles information and how to contact us about your data.

Website information updated: October 2026

Who we are

Neorons Inc. operates this website as part of its international suicide-prevention and inclusion work. For the purposes of applicable data protection law, Neorons Inc. is the data controller.

What we collect

This website does not set cookies, use analytics services, or collect personal data through tracking. Contact email links open your email application. Information is sent to us only when you send an email.

If you contact us, we receive your email address and the information you include in your message. We use this information to respond to your enquiry.

Your display preferences

If you pause decorative motion, this website stores that choice in your browser’s local storage. The setting stays on your device and is not sent to us. It does not identify you.

Photography of participants

Photographs taken during Neorons events are covered by separate consent processes described in our Safeguarding Policy. Published images are stripped of EXIF metadata.

Third parties

We do not share personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. Email enquiries are handled through email services. When you follow an external link, the destination website’s privacy policy applies.

India DPDP Act 2023

We acknowledge the provisions of India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Where our programs collect personal data of participants in India (including children’s data, which requires verifiable parental consent), we comply with applicable requirements. Data subjects may exercise their rights by writing to us.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal data we hold about you. Contact us at neoronsai@gmail.com.
