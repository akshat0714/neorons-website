Everyone deserves to feel heard. Everywhere.

Neorons is a nonprofit working towards a world where suicide prevention is within everyone’s reach. Our mission begins with people: their languages, their communities, and their need for connection.

From inclusive campus programmes to a multilingual conversational AI phone service, we are growing the ways people can connect with our work.
